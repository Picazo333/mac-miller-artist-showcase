ABOUT / ARTIST / HUMAN

More than music.

Mac Miller was a rapper, singer, songwriter, and producer whose work kept changing because he kept changing.

Born Malcolm James McCormick in Pittsburgh, he moved from youthful mixtape energy into increasingly introspective, melodic, and genre-blurring music. Across his career, humor, vulnerability, curiosity, and experimentation became part of the same creative language.

“Different chapters. Same curious soul.”

CAREER / EVOLUTION / EXPERIMENTATION

Different sounds.
Same heartbeat.

2013

Watching Movies with the Sound Off

A darker, stranger, more inward turn: surreal production and questions bigger than the persona.

2015

GO:OD AM

Focused momentum, sharp writing, and a clearer sense of artistic control.

2016

The Divine Feminine

Warmth, intimacy, jazz and soul textures, and a romantic lens on connection.

2018

Swimming

Self-reflection without easy answers: resilience, isolation, growth, and motion.

2020

Circles

A restrained, melodic companion to Swimming, completed and released posthumously.

2025

Balloonerism

An archival project from an earlier creative period, officially released years later.
